## The prompt

```text
Write a short, friendly handoff email for a client whose website we just
launched. Context:

- Client: [business name], contact: [name]
- Site: [URL]
- They can edit in GitCMS: [list, e.g. page copy, photos, blog posts, opening hours]
- We keep: design, new pages, features, integrations
- Changes they make [publish right away / are reviewed by us before going live]
- They sign in at gitcms.dev with Google using the invite we sent

Include a 3-step "your first edit" walkthrough, a clear list of what's theirs
vs ours, and how to request bigger changes. Plain language, no technical terms.
```

## Why it matters

A handoff sets expectations. Writing down who owns what turns "can you add a booking widget?" into a project instead of a favor.
