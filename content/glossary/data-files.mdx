## What lives in them

- Navigation menus and footer links
- Pricing plans and feature lists
- Team members, testimonials, locations
- Site settings like phone numbers and opening hours

Frameworks read them at build time: Hugo has a `data/` directory, Eleventy has global data files, and Astro and Next.js can import JSON or YAML directly.

## Why they matter

On many marketing sites, the copy people most want to change lives in data files, not in blog posts. A pricing change is a data file edit.

## The editing problem

Data files are easy for developers and coding agents and risky for everyone else: one missing comma or wrong indent breaks the build. That's why they're often edited only by developers, which turns every pricing tweak into a ticket.

## In GitCMS

GitCMS edits Markdown and MDX content entries today. Keep structured copy that clients should edit in frontmatter fields of content entries so it's editable in the visual editor.
