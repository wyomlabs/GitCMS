## Example

```text
User-agent: *
Disallow: /app/
Disallow: /api/

Sitemap: https://example.com/sitemap.xml
```

## What it doesn't do

- It doesn't hide pages from search results. A blocked page can still be indexed from links. Use a `noindex` robots meta tag for that.
- It isn't security. Well-behaved crawlers follow it; others ignore it.

## AI crawlers

AI companies publish their own user agents (for example GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot). You can allow or block them individually. Blocking them keeps your content out of some AI systems, but it can also keep you out of their answers. Decide per crawler, deliberately.
