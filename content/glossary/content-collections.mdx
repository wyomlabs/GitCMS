## In frameworks

Astro has built-in content collections with schemas. Other frameworks use folders plus a loader, such as Nuxt Content or an MDX pipeline in Next.js.

## Why they matter for handoff

Copy in collections can be edited safely by non-developers. Copy hardcoded in components can't. Moving editable text into collections is the most important step before handing a site to a client.

## In GitCMS

Each collection becomes a section in the GitCMS sidebar with structured fields. See [how to let clients edit an Astro site](/blog/let-clients-edit-astro-site).
