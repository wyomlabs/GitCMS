## What a build does

- Compiles components and TypeScript
- Renders pages to HTML (for statically generated pages)
- Reads Markdown and MDX content and validates frontmatter
- Optimizes images and bundles assets
- Generates the sitemap and other files

Commands look like `npm run build` or `astro build`, and output a folder such as `dist/` or `.next/`.

## Why content can break a build

On a code-first site, content is input to the build. A missing required field, an invalid date, or a broken MDX tag can fail it, and then nothing deploys. That's why editors need structured fields and validation rather than raw files.

## Build time

Builds usually take seconds to a few minutes. Very large sites use caching or incremental rendering to stay fast.
