## Example

| Type | Fields |
|---|---|
| Blog post | title, description, date, author, tags, body |
| Case study | client, industry, results, quote, body |
| Location page | city, phone, hours, services, body |

## Why it matters

A good content model lets the same content render in many places (cards, pages, feeds) and keeps editors from breaking layouts. A bad one forces copy into the wrong fields or into components.

## On a code-first website

The content model lives in code: a collection schema in Astro, or the frontmatter fields your templates read. Each content type is a folder of files, and each field is a frontmatter key.

## In GitCMS

GitCMS reads your collections and fields and shows them as structured forms, so editors fill in fields instead of editing raw frontmatter.
