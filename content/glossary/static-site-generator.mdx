## How it works

At build time, the generator reads templates and content files and outputs HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The result is fast, cheap to host, and secure.

## With a CMS

A Git-based CMS edits the content files the generator reads. Each published change is a commit that triggers a rebuild. GitCMS works with Astro, Next.js, Hugo, Jekyll, Eleventy, Docusaurus, and more; see [framework setup](/docs/getting-started/frameworks).
