## Example

```xml
<urlset xmlns="http://www.sitemaps.org/schemas/sitemap/0.9">
  <url>
    <loc>https://example.com/blog/opening-hours/</loc>
    <lastmod>2026-10-10</lastmod>
  </url>
</urlset>
```

## What belongs in it

Only canonical, indexable pages: no redirects, no `noindex` pages, no duplicate URLs with and without trailing slashes. Point to it from `robots.txt` and submit it in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

## On code-first sites

Frameworks generate sitemaps at build time (for example `@astrojs/sitemap` or `next-sitemap`), so every new page appears on the next deploy.

## In GitCMS

GitCMS can use your sitemap for internal link suggestions and to match live URLs to entries. See [sitemaps and internal links](/docs/features/sitemaps-internal-links).
