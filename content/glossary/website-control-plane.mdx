## Why the term

In infrastructure, a control plane decides what happens and the data plane does the work. For websites, the repository and your host do the work. Something still has to decide who can change what, which changes are ready, and what goes live.

## What it covers

- Access: who can edit which site
- Workflow: draft, review, and publish stages
- Agents: structured access instead of raw repository credentials
- History: an audit trail of every change

## In GitCMS

GitCMS is the control plane for code-first websites. Developers still change code in the repo; everyone else, people and agents, changes the website through GitCMS.
