## The three layers

| Layer | What it covers | Who usually owns it |
|---|---|---|
| Technical | Crawlability, speed, sitemaps, canonical URLs, rendering | Developers |
| On-page | Titles, descriptions, headings, internal links, content quality | Writers and marketers |
| Off-page | Links and mentions from other sites | Marketing and PR |

## Code-first sites start ahead

Pre-rendered pages, fast hosting, and clean HTML give code-first websites strong technical SEO by default. Search engines and AI crawlers can read the content without running JavaScript.

## Where they fall behind

On-page SEO is content work: rewriting titles, refreshing old posts, adding internal links. If every change needs a developer, it doesn't happen. A CMS that lets marketers and agents make those edits, with review, is what keeps SEO moving after launch. See the [SEO prompts](/prompts) for agent-assisted examples.
