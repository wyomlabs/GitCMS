## Day One vs Day Two

Day One is building the site. Coding agents have made Day One fast and cheap. Day Two is running it: the copy change on a Friday, the new case study before a launch, the agent that rewrote twenty pages overnight.

Website builders bundled Day Two with Day One: you got an admin because the site lived inside the builder. A code-first site has to add it.

## What a Day Two workflow needs

- A visual editor for people who don't use Git
- Review before publish for changes from people and agents
- Roles and per-site access for clients and teams
- History of who changed what

## In GitCMS

GitCMS owns Day Two for code-first websites. "Building the site is Day One. GitCMS is for everything after."
