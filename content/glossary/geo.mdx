## GEO vs AEO

The terms are used almost interchangeably. When people distinguish them:

- **AEO** focuses on being the quoted source for a specific question.
- **GEO** focuses on how AI systems describe your brand overall: which category they place you in, which competitors they list beside you, and whether the facts are right.

## What influences it

- **Consistency** of how your own pages describe you
- **Third-party mentions** in directories, reviews, comparisons, and communities
- **Crawlable, current pages** with clear facts (pricing, features, audience)
- **Structured data** that names your organization and product

## Measuring it

There's no Search Console for AI answers yet. A practical method: run a fixed set of questions your customers ask through ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google each month, and log whether you're mentioned and how you're described.
