## Example

```md
# Example Co.

> Example Co. is a plumbing company serving Austin, Texas, with 24/7 emergency call-outs.

## Services
- [Emergency plumbing](https://example.com/emergency/): Response in under an hour
- [Water heaters](https://example.com/water-heaters/): Repair and installation

## Docs
- [Pricing](https://example.com/pricing.md)
```

The proposal is at [llmstxt.org](https://llmstxt.org). Some sites also publish `llms-full.txt` with full content, and Markdown versions of pages.

## Does it matter?

Support varies across AI systems, and it's not a ranking signal. It's cheap to generate from data you already have, and it gives agents and tools a clean map of your site. Treat it as a nice-to-have, not a strategy.

## On code-first sites

Generate it at build time from your pages and content collections, with the same one-sentence description you use everywhere else.
