## Unstructured vs structured

An unstructured page is one long rich-text body. A structured page separates the parts: `heroHeadline`, `heroSubheadline`, `ctaLabel`, `features[]`, and so on.

## Why it helps

- **Reuse**: the same headline appears on the page, in a card, and in a social preview
- **Safety**: editors change a field, not the layout
- **SEO and answer engines**: clear fields map to titles, descriptions, and structured data
- **AI agents**: an agent can update one field across fifty pages reliably

## On a code-first website

Structured content lives in frontmatter fields and data files. Designing those fields well, before handing a site to a client, is what makes it editable without a developer.
