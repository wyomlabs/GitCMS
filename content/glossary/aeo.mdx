## How answer engines use your pages

An answer engine searches the web, retrieves passages that look relevant, and writes an answer, often citing its sources. It quotes **passages**, not whole pages. Your goal is to be the passage it picks.

## Patterns that get quoted

- **A direct answer first.** Open with a 40–60 word answer to the question in the heading.
- **One consistent definition.** Describe your product the same way on every page, so engines learn one description.
- **Question-shaped headings.** "What is…", "How do I…", "X vs Y".
- **Tables and lists** for comparisons and steps.
- **Freshness.** Visible "last reviewed" dates and current facts.
- **Corroboration.** Other sites describing you the same way.

## AEO vs SEO

They overlap heavily: answer engines lean on search indexes to find pages. The difference is the unit of success. SEO is measured in clicks; AEO is measured in citations and mentions, which often produce no click at all.
