## Why they matter

- **Discovery**: crawlers find new pages by following links
- **Context**: anchor text tells search engines what the target is about
- **Priority**: pages with more internal links signal importance
- **Readers**: good links keep people moving through related content

## Doing it well

- Use descriptive anchor text ("how to hand off a client site"), not "click here"
- Link from older, established pages to new ones
- Fix or remove links to pages that moved
- Don't stuff: three to five genuinely useful links per article is plenty

## At scale

Adding links across hundreds of posts is tedious, which is why it rarely happens. It's a good job for an agent: suggest links using the site's real URLs and submit them for review. GitCMS uses your sitemap for [internal link suggestions](/docs/features/sitemaps-internal-links).
