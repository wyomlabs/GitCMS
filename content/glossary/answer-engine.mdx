## Examples

ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Claude with web search, Microsoft Copilot, and Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode.

## How they choose sources

Most answer engines run a search (often against Bing's or Google's index, or their own crawler), retrieve candidate pages, extract passages, and synthesize an answer. Some run many sub-queries for one question, which is why you may see long, oddly phrased queries in Search Console.

## What it means for websites

- Pages still need to be indexed and crawlable, so SEO still matters
- Clear, quotable passages beat long introductions
- Being mentioned on other sites counts, because engines cross-check claims
- Clicks may drop even when visibility rises
