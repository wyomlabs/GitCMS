## Example

```text
company/
├── apps/web/        the product
├── sites/marketing/ marketing website
├── sites/docs/      documentation site
└── packages/ui/     shared components
```

## Why teams use them

Shared components, one place for dependencies, and atomic changes across projects. A design system change can update the app and the marketing site in one commit.

## The content challenge

With several sites in one repository, editors need to work on their site only, with the right collections and permissions, without seeing the rest of the codebase.

## In GitCMS

A repository can hold several sites, each set up as its own GitCMS site with its own configuration, collections, and access. Editors see the site they're invited to, not the monorepo.
