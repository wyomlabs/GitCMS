## What it shows

- **Performance**: queries, pages, clicks, impressions, click-through rate, and average position, for up to 16 months
- **Indexing**: which pages are indexed, and why others aren't
- **Sitemaps**: submission status and discovered URLs
- **URL inspection**: how Google sees a specific page

## Reports worth checking monthly

- Queries with many impressions but low click-through rate: titles and descriptions to rewrite
- Pages ranking on page 2 (positions 11–20): content worth improving
- Duplicate URL pairs: canonical or redirect problems
- Long, question-like queries: signs of AI systems searching for answers

## Getting the data out

The interface caps tables at 1,000 rows. The Search Console API returns more and lets you combine query and page data, which is how agents and dashboards analyze it.

Also set up **Bing Webmaster Tools**: it can import your Search Console setup, and some answer engines rely on Bing's index.
