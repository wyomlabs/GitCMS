## How to compress images

1. Drop images onto the upload area or click it to choose files.
2. Pick a quality and a maximum width. A quality around 80 and a width of 1920 px suits most blog and documentation images.
3. Compare each result with the original, then download images one at a time or all at once.

## Why WebP

WebP files are usually 25–35% smaller than JPEGs at the same visual quality, and much smaller than PNG screenshots. Smaller images load faster, which helps Core Web Vitals and keeps repositories that store media in Git from growing quickly.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are my images uploaded?

No. Compression runs in your browser, so your images never leave your device.

### What happens if WebP isn't smaller?

The tool keeps the original file and marks it as kept, so you never download a larger image than you started with.

### Can GitCMS compress images when I add them to a post?

Yes. [GitCMS](/) compresses images when you upload them in the editor, then commits them to your repository.
