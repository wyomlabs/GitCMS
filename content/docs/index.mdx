GitCMS is the CMS for code-first and AI-built websites. It gives your team, your clients, and AI agents one safe place to edit, review, and publish website changes, while everything stays in your site's repository.

GitCMS reads and writes your Git repository directly. Every save is a real commit. No sync layer, no shadow database.

## Features

- **[Markdown Editor](/docs/features/markdown-editor)** — Notion-like editing, slash commands, frontmatter forms, media, and beta custom SSG component support.
- **[Collections & Fields](/docs/features/collections)** — Content schemas with field types and validation.
- **[Localization (i18n)](/docs/features/localization-i18n)** — Manage multilingual content with locale-aware paths and defaults.
- **[Media Library](/docs/features/media-management)** — Upload, organize, and reuse media from your repository or object storage.
- **[Sitemaps, Internal Links & Live URLs](/docs/features/sitemaps-internal-links)** — Use your sitemap for internal link suggestions, live URL matching, and locale-aware page lookup.
- **[Google Search Console & Bing](/docs/features/search-connections)** — Connect search accounts, link each site to its property, or ask a client to share theirs.
- **[Search Data in the Editor](/docs/features/search-data)** — Index status, clicks, and what to improve on every entry, plus the Search page.
- **[Editorial Workflow](/docs/features/editorial-workflow)** — Ideas → Draft → Review → Published on the content board, without Git jargon in the default experience.
- **[Roles & Site Access](/docs/features/access-control)** — Manage editors, reviewers, viewers, selected-site access, and publish rights.
