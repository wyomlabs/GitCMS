## v1.1.0

GitCMS v1.1 makes everyday content work smoother. Finding files is faster, review tasks keep their context, the content board is easier to read, and the app feels calmer while you move around.

## What's new

**Open content faster with Cmd+K**

Search collections, entries, and recent files from one command palette. It works in the app and in public GitCMS site views.
