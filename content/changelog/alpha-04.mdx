## Alpha Update 04

GitCMS is now much more usable for teams.

This update focuses on collaboration: inviting people in, managing members, and moving toward clearer site-level access instead of assuming every user should see and do everything.

## What changed

- Expanded team invitations and member management
- Improved workspace-based collaboration flows
- Added the foundations for scoped site access and safer permissions
- Started aligning the product around preview access and controlled editing

## Why it mattered

GitCMS is no longer just for a single technical owner. This makes it easier to bring in marketers, editors, and collaborators without exposing the whole product or repo surface to everyone.
