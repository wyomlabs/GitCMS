## Alpha Update 03

GitCMS starts experimenting with its first MCP workflows for ChatGPT and Claude.

This update focuses on experimentation. GitCMS begins testing how conversational drafting, editor context, and content workflows could work inside AI clients before the MCP app model is fully shaped.

## What changed

- Started exploring the first GitCMS MCP workflows in ChatGPT and Claude
- Tested early UX for turning ChatGPT and Claude into content agents
- Began shaping GitCMS around conversational content workflows
- Laid the groundwork for the MCP app model that follows later

## Why it mattered

This sets the direction for GitCMS AI workflows. Instead of treating AI as a separate writing tool, GitCMS starts moving toward a model where drafting can happen inside the real content workflow.
