## Alpha Update 06

GitCMS now turns ChatGPT and Claude into content agents.

The MCP app now connects conversational drafting with the actual content workflow. Instead of acting like a loose AI editing experiment, GitCMS can now guide the model through content tasks, in-chat writing, and review-aware content work.

## What changed

- GitCMS now frames ChatGPT and Claude as content agents instead of generic AI tools
- Content work can start from brainstorming and move into real content tasks
- The in-chat app is now better aligned with drafting, editing, and review flow
- GitCMS now makes the conversational workflow clearer for real content operations

## Why it mattered

This is the point where the AI story becomes product-shaped. ChatGPT and Claude are no longer just places where ideas start. With GitCMS, they become part of a real content workflow that still stays grounded in your site structure, editor, and review process.
